# Global Salary Analysis — UK, USA, Australia, Canada, China

**Tools:** PostgreSQL, Power BI  
**Dataset:** [Salary by Job Title and Country — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/amirmahdiabbootalebi/salary-by-job-title-and-country)

## Business Problem
HR teams need to understand what drives salary differences across countries,
job titles, education levels, and years of experience — to benchmark salaries
and make competitive job offers.

## What I Did
- Loaded 6,704 salary records across 5 countries into PostgreSQL
- Identified top 10 highest paying jobs per country
- Analysed how education level and years of experience affect salary
- Compared average salaries by gender across all countries

## Key Findings
- Data Scientist avg salary in UK: $165,898
- PhD holders earn significantly more than Bachelor graduates
- 10+ years of experience correlates with ~60% higher salary
- Software Engineer Manager is top-paying role across all countries
